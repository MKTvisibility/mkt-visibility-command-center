# Approved campaign email workflow

The user visually reviews public business profiles, images, contacts and previous correspondence. They prepare an exact email and check Approved to Send. This stores one immutable approval in `public.cc_email_approvals`; no Gmail credential is stored in Command Center and no background sender runs.

## User workflow

Open a business profile → Next email & approval. The four campaign cards show verified message history and manual contact evidence, independently of general checklist completion. Listing Welcome already contacted suggests Business Story; an advanced conversation or human response suggests Custom email. An unclear historical send requires classification in its existing message record before a standard campaign approval. Custom email is available for a deliberate follow-up, correction or response. Bounced addresses and stopped outreach block approval. Existing messages remain intact.

Choose Prepare, review/edit sender, recipient, subject and full body, optionally add notes, confirm visual review and check Approved to Send. Save approval. One active approval per business prevents two campaigns being queued together. Uncheck the saved approval to withdraw it. Changing approved copy requires withdrawal and a new approval; the old record is retained.

The campaign dashboard has Approved to Send counts and an alphabetical approved queue including custom emails. Profile, task, listing, purchase or message changes mark the approval Review again. The backend checks current Do Not Contact state on approval and claim as well. No historical client is automatically approved by migration.

## Permissions and verification

Approved workspace editors can approve or revoke, viewers can read, anonymous users cannot read or change approvals. Dispatch RPCs are service-role/admin only. All approval changes use the existing provenance and audit triggers. Never expose a service key or Gmail credential to frontend code. Database tests exercise permissions, stale contexts, duplicate prevention, claims, immutable wording and atomic idempotent recording. UI tests exercise the actual approval/withdrawal flow with no email sent.
