# Business events

Open a business’s **Edit & publish the live business page → Events & calendar** box. Choose an existing date or **Add event**, enter the title, dates, Mountain times, location, description and organizer/ticket link, and upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image or paste an HTTPS image URL.

**Show on business calendar** controls public visibility. **Spotlight on Events Page** additionally includes that visible event on Loving Longmont’s main Events page. Save the event draft, preview it, then publish events. Story and photo edits have their own publishing controls. Hidden and removed events disappear publicly only after events are published again. Publishing an empty calendar removes its public events while retaining the business profile.

The business calendar groups dates by month. The main Events page groups spotlighted dates by business and month, with expandable details and search. Past events disappear using America/Denver dates and end times. An event with no end time stays visible through its final day. Visitors should confirm availability and registration requirements at the organizer’s link.

Event drafts are private to approved workspace members, with writes and publishing restricted to editors. Preview signatures and draft versions reject stale changes. Public snapshots contain only enabled events. The separate event tables do not change saved business stories, logos or galleries.

The October 17–December 31, 2026 source review covered 137 distinct listed businesses and organizations and produced 806 dated occurrences from 38 hosts. Public organizer calendars, linked registration pages, municipal event feeds and downtown event listings supplied dates. Undated announcements, outdated calendars, canceled and explicitly members-only events were excluded. A business without a confirmed public date was left without an imported calendar. Social-only and inaccessible listings may have events that could not be verified. The checked event records are in `event-calendar-seed-2026.json`; the public site retains the source review and verified static fallback listings for connection outages.

## Event venues and party planning

The general Loving Longmont Events page business spotlight section is titled **Event Venues & Party Planning**. Select **Events → Include**, then choose **Event Venues & Party Planning** in its section menu, preview the business page, and publish. This uses the existing general `events.html` placement; it requires no separate venue checkbox. Month selections still target month sections, and dated event spotlights remain in the separate Events & calendar editor.

The initial venue review selected 19 existing listings with verified private-booking offerings, including Bricks' event/work space, farm and historic wedding venues, art parties, climbing parties, recreation packages, and private rink rentals. Six previously static venue profiles were connected using their existing public descriptions and photos, with verified booking offerings shown as services. No independent dedicated party planner was confirmed in the current listings. Add those businesses here when verified. The source audit is saved alongside the public Events page. Crackpots' previously saved domain was parked for sale; its website link was corrected to the official ecrackpots.com site.
