What this is and why we use it

What is agent memory? Saved instructions, original research, checkpoints, decisions and activity records that help AI assistants continue work across sessions.

How does it work? Agents consult their saved instructions and last verified checkpoint, retrieve relevant sources, perform tasks, then record what was attempted and what was actually verified.

Why use it? To reduce duplicated research, lost progress, forgotten requirements and unnecessary processing.

How it helps: More consistent work, better handoffs, auditable activity, approval controls and potential cost savings.

How it can hurt: Summaries may omit important facts; saved information can become stale or incorrect; retrieval and storage have costs; weak access controls can expose private client information. Keep original sources, verify outcomes, limit permissions and review costs.

Important: A task is not published or sent just because an agent says it is. Require evidence from the live website or messaging provider.
