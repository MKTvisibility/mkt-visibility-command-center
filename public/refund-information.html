Cancellation & refund information

You may cancel your order and request a refund within 72 hours of payment. The period begins when payment is made, not when you submit a service inquiry.

Any deduction will be limited to the original Square processing fees actually retained on that payment, where permitted by law. We do not add a separate cancellation fee. Any rights required by applicable law remain unchanged.

How to request cancellation

Email mktvisibility@gmail.com with your name, order or invoice number, payment date and cancellation request. You may also use the cancellation form in your secure report portal. Keep a copy of your request and submission time.

Your payment confirmation should state your exact cancellation deadline. Refund processing time depends on Square and your financial institution. We will confirm the refund amount and any actual retained processing fee before processing.

Choosing services in the report portal sends an inquiry. It does not charge your card or begin the 72-hour period.
