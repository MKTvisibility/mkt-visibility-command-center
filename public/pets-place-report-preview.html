Google listing & reviews

4 / 5

The hospital prominently advertises its 2025 Longmont Top 5 animal-hospital recognition and links to review destinations. Its address and telephone number are consistent on the official website and the Great Pet Care directory. The live Google Business Profile review total and owner-response frequency were not independently verified, so the 4/5 channel score reflects the existing visibility assessment rather than a Google customer star rating. Facebook

3 / 5

LinkedIn

Needs verification

Priority: business information accuracy. The public LinkedIn company page displays 731 N. Main St., while the hospital's official website and another veterinary directory list 1011 Coffman Street. Update LinkedIn's address and company description to reflect the current location, services and team. The LinkedIn page also names Dr. Joan Primeau, while the hospital announced her September 30 retirement; verify staff information before updating.

Recommended next steps

01 · Align business information

Correct the LinkedIn street address to 1011 Coffman Street and confirm other directory listings use the same location, telephone number, business hours and website. Verify the staff information following Dr. Primeau's announced retirement.

02 · Strengthen local discovery

Expand service-specific discovery for dentistry, acupuncture, laser therapy, regenerative medicine and pet travel certificates. Refresh Google Business Profile photos and review-response practices, and make booking links prominent.

03 · Measure outcomes

Connect website analytics and Command Center tracking to measure visitors, page views, appointment-button clicks, form submissions, directory profile opens and phone clicks for a clearly defined reporting period.

04 · Track results over time

Review performance monthly to measure search visibility, website engagement, and appointment inquiries.
